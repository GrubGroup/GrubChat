# Reflection #5

Pod Members: **Della Lee, Daniel Lam, Audrey Dequito, Miguel Cuevas**

## Reflection Questions

- How was the pacing of the capstone project? (i.e too slow, just right, too fast)
  The pacing was too slow, especially when it came to the week where we needed to connect the backend to the frontend and deploy the web application using Render and Fly.io

- To what extent did your plan change over the course of development? Knowing that you know now, what would you do differently if you were starting over?
  The plan didn't really change but the scope of the project changed due to limited timing from the delays of the project development where we had to connect the frontend and backend. With the experience we've gained, we would have focused either scoped down on the project or put more time into the connection of the frontend and backend during development. Focusing on the deployment and connection earlier in the week vs taking the whole week like we did.

- How helpful were the labs and weekly assignments in preparing you to create a capstone project? Be specific, what topics do you still have questions about that may or may not have been covered?
  For our specific project, they weren't as helpful but the most helpful one would be the student store assignment since our project was built off using Postgresql and Prisma transactions. We have questions mainly on system architecture, building a project for scale, and unit testing during development.

- When planning for the capstone project, which resources were the most helpful? (i.e mentors, instructors & TAs, ideation process, pod syncs, wireframes, sprint planning, bug bash, practice demo day, etc.)
  The most helpful things where the mentors which gave us direct feedback, claude for expanding on general ideas, and pod syncs to keep a consistent development timeline.
